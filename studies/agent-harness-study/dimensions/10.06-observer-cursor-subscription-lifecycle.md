# Dimension 10.06: Observer Cursor and Subscription Lifecycle

## Purpose

Study cursor validity, replay boundaries, subscription races, observer abandonment, stream completion, and history lifetime.

## Steps

1. Identify snapshot, cursor, subscription, and replay APIs.
2. Trace subscription concurrent with event publication.
3. Inspect slow, disconnected, repeated, and abandoned observers.
4. Determine cursor expiry and retained-history boundaries.
5. Check terminal stream completion and post-completion replay.

## Evidence to Capture

- Cursor and subscription types
- History storage and replay queries
- Notification and buffering logic
- Cursor expiry and retention rules
- Race, slow-consumer, abandonment, and completion tests

## Questions

1. Can subscribe-versus-publish lose an event?
2. Is replay inclusive, exclusive, or sequence based?
3. Can one observer block execution or another observer?
4. When does a subscription end?
5. What makes a cursor invalid or unrecoverable?
