## Executive Summary

No source can answer "can the system produce a compliance report for a given run?" with a single, signed, reproducible artifact. All five emit fragmented, machine-readable slices — traces/spans, violation dicts, mutation rows, decision events, run-state snapshots — but none binds *what happened + what was checked + what controls applied + who approved when + retention guarantee* into one ledger.

The mature pattern is **generic observability or mutation auditing repurposed for governance**: OPA's decision logs (`v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:49-76`), Langfuse's `AuditLog` table (`packages/shared/prisma/schema.prisma:852-876`), agent-framework's OTel spans (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:422-545`), and openai-agents-sdk's `Trace`/`Span` exports (`src/agents/tracing/traces.py:380-387`, `src/agents/tracing/spans.py:396-422`) are well-typed and JSON-serializable. OpenHands contributes only a client-assembled activity-log export (`src/utils/automation-activity-log-export.ts:12-27`) over remote state.

Convergence: evidence is in-memory or volatile by default, approval timestamps are absent in every source, policy-decision records are partial, SIEM integration is DIY via generic OTLP/HTTP/S3, and retention for governance evidence is unenforced (or deliberately indefinite). Divergence reflects product shape: Langfuse as SaaS platform goes furthest toward durability and export; OPA as stateless policy engine toward rich decision provenance but volatile delivery; the two agent SDKs toward tracing-bus evidence with versioned checkpoints; OpenHands as frontend toward read-only views.

Treat current state as **observability exhaust, not governance ledger**. Building a compliance report requires joining 2-4 stores externally, with no signing, immutability, or approval attestation provided out of box.

## Core Thesis

Governance evidence generation across these harnesses follows an **observability-first, governance-second** trajectory: teams ship structured tracing or audit logging for debugging/operations, then discover compliance requires a *separate* ledger with timestamps, actors, policy versions, approval chains, immutability, and retention — none of which the tracing primitive provides without explicit extension. The result is five variants of the same gap: strong machine-readability for *what happened*, weak or absent guarantees for *what was checked under which policy when, by whom, and for how long evidence must survive*.

Maturity correlates with persistence boundary: the platform that owns storage (Langfuse) retains audit rows indefinitely but omits deny-logging and signing; the stateless engine (OPA) captures the richest per-decision provenance but drops it on bounded buffers; the library SDKs (agent-framework, openai-agents-sdk) persist approvals/conversation in session stores but keep policy violations volatile and timestamp-free; the frontend (OpenHands) owns no persistence at all.

## Approach Models

Three clusters emerge, shaped by where persistence lives and whether the system is a library, engine, or platform.

### Cluster 1: Tracing-Bus Frameworks (agent-framework, openai-agents-sdk)

Evidence is spans under a trace, fanned out via a processor interface to an exporter. Governance reuses observability plumbing.

- **agent-framework:** OTel semantic conventions (`OtelAttr` `gen_ai.*`/`workflow.*` at `observability.py:223-362`), `_create_otlp_exporters` gRPC/HTTP (`observability.py:422-545`), `_get_exporters_from_env` (`observability.py:548-638`). Security policy violations sit *outside* the span stream as an in-memory `audit_log` (`security.py:2163-2172`). `ToolApprovalState` is durable in `AgentSession.state` (`_harness/_tool_approval.py:248-275`) — approvals survive restart, violations do not. No `generate_compliance_report()` (`python/scripts/sample_validation/report.py:15` is CI-only).
- **openai-agents-sdk:** `Trace.export()` (`traces.py:380-387`) and `Span.export()` (`spans.py:396-422`) with `span_data` hierarchy (`span_data.py:11-451`). Guardrail tripwires (`result.py:325-333`, `run_state.py:1778-1812`) and `ToolApprovalItem` interruptions (`run_state.py:826-849`) are checkpointed in `RunState` v1.17 (`run_state.py:182-218`). `TracingProcessor`/`TracingExporter` (`processor_interface.py:9-142`) + `BackendSpanExporter` to `https://api.openai.com/v1/traces/ingest` (`tracing/processors.py:44-235`) is the sole shipped sink; `BatchTraceProcessor` queue 8192/schedule 5s drops evidence on overflow.

Shared traits: pluggable SIEM seam without shipped SIEM mapping; sensitive-data gating that can starve evidence; mutable approval maps without event timestamps.

### Cluster 2: Policy Decision Side-Channel (opa)

Evidence is a post-evaluation log stream from a stateless engine. Every Data/Query decision builds `server.Info` (`v1/server/buffer.go:18-43`) into `EventV1` (`v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:49-76`) with `timestamp`, `decision_id`, `bundles[Revision]` (`v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:79-81`), `input`/`result`/`nd_builtin_cache`, and `metrics`. Provenance (`v1/server/types/types.go:225-238`) and bundle status timestamps (`v1/plugins/bundle/status.go:24-39`) provide management evidence. Delivery is buffered (size vs event, `v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:612-629`) with adaptive gzip chunking (`v1/plugins/logs/encoder.go:49-101`) and generic HTTP upload (`v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:1143-1162`). No approval workflow, no tool log, no retention, no native SIEM transformer — operators bring their own endpoint.

### Cluster 3: Platform Audit Ledger vs Stateless Viewer (langfuse, openhands)

- **langfuse:** Central `auditLog()` helper (`web/src/features/audit-logs/auditLog.ts:86-155`) normalized across 50+ tRPC handlers writes `AuditLog` rows (`schema.prisma:852-876`) with `createdAt`, actor (`USER`|`API_KEY`), org/project roles at action time, `resourceType` (45+ literals at `auditLog.ts:7-52`), `action`, `before`/`after` strings. Readers `auditLogs.all` / `allByOrg` (`web/src/server/api/routers/auditLogs.ts:78-277`) + EE pages + `BatchExportTableName.AuditLogs` via `DatabaseReadStream` paginated `orderBy createdAt desc` limited by `cutoffCreatedAt` (`getDatabaseReadStream.ts:635-673`) to S3. Durable but siloed: agent `InAppAgentEvent`/`InAppAgentRun` (`schema.prisma:263-313`) and `AutomationExecution` (`worker/src/queues/webhooks.ts:334-348`) live separately — no join.
- **openhands:** Frontend owns no ledger. It projects backend state: paginated `EventService.searchEvents` (`src/api/event-service/event-service.api.ts:97-182`) into `useEventStore` (`src/stores/use-event-store.ts:54-223`), trajectory download (`src/api/conversation-service/conversation-service.api.ts:54-63`), and client-assembled `AutomationRunExportRow` dump (`src/utils/automation-activity-log-export.ts:113-170`) with `run_id`/`trigger`/`start_time`/`end_time`/`duration_seconds`/`status`/`conversation_url`/`cost`. No policy, approval, SIEM, or retention code; docs delegate to future DefenseClaw sidecar (`docs/DefenseClaw.md:211-282`).

Why divergence is rational: a library defers storage/retention to the host; a policy engine optimizes for per-decision delivery; a SaaS platform can centralize audit but must tier it as an entitlement; a frontend cannot own durability.

## Pattern Catalog

### 1. Structured Evidence Exports as `dict`/`JSON`/`CSV` (All sources)

*Problem:* SIEMs and auditors need machine-parseable evidence without custom parsers.
*Demonstrated by:* agent-framework `ContentLabel.to_dict`, `ToolApprovalState.to_dict` (`security.py:185-209`, `_tool_approval.py:120-215`) + OTel attrs; langfuse typed `AuditLogRow` + JSON/CSV export (`auditLogs.ts:29-76`, `getDatabaseReadStream.ts:652-668`); OPA `eventToAttrs`/`eventToFields` (`plugin.go:1201-1239`, `1287-1363`) + gzip JSON array; openai-agents-sdk `Trace.export`/`Span.export`/`RunState.to_json` (`traces.py:152`, `spans.py:181`, `run_state.py:1704-2288`); openhands `AutomationRunExportRow` CSV/JSON (`automation-activity-log-export.ts:48-64`).
*Why it works:* Standard JSON + explicit schema lets any collector ingest; pagination/cutoff makes exports reproducible.
*When to copy:* Always — evidence that isn't JSON-serializable can't reach a SIEM.
*When risky:* Untyped `Any` payloads (openai-agents-sdk `output_info`, `mcp_data`) or truncated fields (opa NDBCache strip, SDK 100 kB truncation) silently lose fidelity. Normalize with versioned schemas.

### 2. Pluggable Processor/Exporter Seam for Generic SIEM Transport (agent-framework, OPA, openai-agents-sdk, langfuse)

*Problem:* No single SIEM covers all enterprises; evidence must fan out without forking core.
*Demonstrated by:* agent-framework `_create_otlp_exporters` + env parsing (`observability.py:422-638`) (OTLP to any collector); OPA `Logger` / `plugins.LoggerPlugin` (`plugin.go:38-43`, `800-818`) plus generic HTTP `uploadChunk` (`plugin.go:1143-1162`); openai-agents-sdk `TracingProcessor` ABC + `SynchronousMultiTracingProcessor` (`processor_interface.py:9-142`, `provider.py:93-220`); langfuse webhook `executeHttpAction` with HMAC + blob export (`worker/src/queues/webhooks.ts:199-319`, `handleBlobStorageIntegrationProjectJob.ts:1-`).
*Why it works:* Decouples evaluation from delivery; operators wire OTel Collector/Splunk HEC/Sentinel themselves.
*When to copy:* When shipping a library or neutral engine.
*When overkill/risky:* Without a reference SIEM exporter, every deployer reinvents field mapping. Provide at least one `FileSpanExporter`/`OTLPExporter` + ECS/OCSF mapping and an integration test. All sources except OPA's console plugin ship zero native SIEM connectors — grep for `SIEM|siem|CEF|LEEF` returns zero hits in core.

### 3. Before/After Snapshot Capture (Langfuse canonical; agent-framework partial)

*Problem:* Reconstructing "what changed" requires change-data-capture, not just "an action occurred".
*Demonstrated by:* Langfuse `auditLog()` JSON-stringifies `before`/`after` at call time (`auditLog.ts:88-93`) alongside actor+role; used to fix mis-typed `resourceType` via migration (`worker/src/backgroundMigrations/patchLLMToolAndLLLMSchemaAuditLogs.ts:40-102`) and verified byte-identical through export (`batchExport.test.ts:1675-1695`). Agent-framework logs violation dicts with `type`/`function`/`context_label`/`reason` (`security.py:1998-2046`) but not full payload diffs.
*Why it works:* Snapshots survive target-row deletion; `No FK` (`schema.prisma:851` comment) preserves evidence after principal deletion.
*When to copy:* Any mutating API needing governance.
*Risk:* Plain `String?` storage (`schema.prisma:866-867`) without signing is mutable; langfuse `updatedAt` implies rows can be updated.

### 4. Versioned / Append-Only Checkpoint for Reproducibility (openai-agents-sdk strongest; agent-framework, Langfuse partial)

*Problem:* Evidence must survive restart and allow deterministic replay or resume.
*Demonstrated by:* openai-agents-sdk `RunState` `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION="1.17"` (`run_state.py:182-218`) with `to_json`/`from_json`/`from_string` (`run_state.py:1704-2267`) and `result.to_state()` (`result.py:541-588`). agent-framework `FileHistoryProvider` append-only JSONL/msgpack (`_sessions.py:2172-2250`, `:2423`) and `ToolApprovalState` in `AgentSession.state` (`_tool_approval.py:248-275`). Langfuse `InAppAgentEvent.sequenceNumber` (`schema.prisma:270`) total-orders per `runId`; `DatabaseReadStream` deterministic `orderBy createdAt desc` (`getDatabaseReadStream.ts:645-646`) with `cutoffCreatedAt`.
*Why it works:* Caller-managed persistence still benefits from schema evolution and stable sort.
*When to copy:* For any pause/resume or HITL approval flow.
*Risk:* Mutable approval maps (openai-agents-sdk `_approvals: dict`, agent-framework `audit_log: list`) without append-only event log lose history.

### 5. Bounded Buffer with Drop-Oldest + Telemetry, and Stale-Guard for Retention (OPA, openai-agents-sdk, langfuse)

*Problem:* Evidence volume must not OOM the host or delete under a stale policy.
*Demonstrated by:* OPA `ReportingConfig{BufferSizeLimitBytes/Events}` 0/unlimited/10000 default (`plugin.go:283-293`) with FIFO `logBuffer` (`buffer.go:32-57`) dropping oldest and `decision_logs_dropped_*` counters (`plugin.go:274-277`); encoder scale-up/scale-down (`encoder.go:94-101`). openai-agents-sdk `BatchTraceProcessor(max_queue_size=8192)` drops on `queue.Full` with warning (`tracing/processors.py:603-604`) and 5s shutdown flush (`setup.py:10-22`). Langfuse re-reads `project.retentionDays` inside the job, skips if `null/0`, warns if `currentRetention !== queued retention` (`handleDataRetentionProcessingJob.ts:26-56`).
*Why it works:* Prevents retention-disable race from becoming data loss.
*When to copy:* Any periodic purge or buffered delivery.
*Risk:* Counters alone are invisible if metrics and logs diverge (OPA); dropping without dead-letter loses compliance evidence.

### 6. Policy-Driven Masking/Redaction and Sensitive-Data Gating (OPA canonical; others partial)

*Problem:* Evidence must not leak PII into SIEM.
*Demonstrated by:* OPA `maskEvent`/`dropEvent` via `/system/log/mask` + `/system/log/drop` Rego refs (`plugin.go:1048-1141`), with `masked`/`erased` pointers retained. agent-framework `enable_sensitive_data` / `ENABLE_SENSITIVE_DATA` (`observability.py:921-925`) gates message events and `emit_tool_call_attributes` (`observability.py:928-934`). openai-agents-sdk `DONT_LOG_MODEL_DATA`/`DONT_LOG_TOOL_DATA` (`logger.py:1-266`, `processors.py:31-42`) redacts payloads to IDs/timing.
*Why it works:* Enforcement at emission avoids downstream scrubbing.
*Risk:* Misconfigured mask silently degrades evidence hygiene but does not fail closed (`plugin.go:766-789`).

### 7. Secure Externalization Hardening (Langfuse)

*Problem:* Bulk export / webhook delivery must be safe for governance pipelines.
*Demonstrated by:* Langfuse `fetchWithSecureRedirects`, `whitelistFromEnv`, `validateWebhookURL`, sensitive-header stripping, HMAC `createSignatureHeader` (`worker/src/queues/webhooks.ts:244-268,535-541`), redirect-chain audit logging (`webhooks.ts:280-289`), exponential `backOff(4)`.
*When to copy:* Any webhook-fed SIEM or Blob Storage export; apply even when evidence schema is generic.

## Tradeoffs

**Reusing observability for governance vs dedicated ledger.** Benefit: immediate SIEM reach via OTLP/HTTP and unified `OtelAttr`/`span_data` schemas; cost: policy decisions live outside spans (agent-framework violations not in spans; OPA status separate from decision logs; Langfuse RBAC denies absent). Best fit: observability-native startups. Failure mode: SIEM shows tool ran but not *why it was blocked*. Alternatives: Langfuse's separate `AuditLog` table and OPA's `EventV1` dedicated schema — more complete for their domain, still siloed.

**Library flexibility (caller-owned storage) vs platform guarantee.** Benefit: fit any stack (agent-framework `InMemory|File|Redis|CosmosHistoryProvider`; openai-agents-sdk caller-held `RunState`); cost: framework cannot guarantee immutability, signing, or retention — durability equals host's store config. Failure mode: `FileHistoryProvider` indefinite retention leaks PII, `RedisHistoryProvider max_messages=0` retains nothing. Langfuse's owned store is the counterpoint: guarantees audit retention (indefinite) but cannot enforce a bounded policy without manual purge.

**Pluggability vs out-of-box governance.** Benefit: `Logger`/`TracingProcessor`/OTLP interfaces cover any SIEM; cost: operators build collector + field mapping + retention themselves. All sources ship at most one hardcoded sink (OpenAI ingest, generic HTTP, OTLP env). Failure: enterprise needing on-prem Splunk/ELK writes and operates its own exporter with no reference implementation.

**Privacy gating vs forensic completeness.** Benefit: `enable_sensitive_data`, `DONT_LOG_MODEL_DATA`, Rego mask/drop prevent PII spill; cost: default evidence lacks inputs/outputs/tool args (`observability.py:928-934` requires opt-in; `processors.py:32` redacts to IDs). Audit debates require deliberate opt-in that many deployments omit.

**Mutable map vs append-only ledger for approvals.** Benefit: `dict` `_approvals`/`ToolApprovalState` enables fast `is_tool_approved` checks and simple resume (`run_context.py:612`, `_tool_approval.py:248-275`); cost: "who approved what when" history is lost — four of five sources lack `approved_at`/`approved_by`. Failure: stale approval replay after policy change (`security.py:1771` re-requests but original violation reason only in old audit entry).

**Bounded buffers with silent drop vs backpressure.** Benefit: protects latency (OPA FIFO, SDK `queue.put_nowait`); cost: violates governance invariant "evidence must not be dropped." `decision_logs_dropped_*` and `logger.warning` are observable only if metrics/logs reach the same SIEM — otherwise silent loss.

**Indefinite audit retention vs governed lifecycle.** Langfuse explicitly excludes `audit_logs` from `retentionDays` purge (`handleDataRetentionProcessingJob.ts:94-99`). Benefit: evidence outlives traces for future audit; cost: unbounded growth and no GDPR purge or WORM hold without out-of-band process. Mirrors library extreme of no retention at all.

## Decision Guide

**Choose per product shape, then close the same four gaps (report, timestamps, SIEM mapping, retention).**

- If building an **agent SDK/library** (agent-framework / openai-agents-sdk model): ship a `TracingProcessor` seam but also add an `EvidenceReport` builder that joins `trace_id` → spans + guardrail outcomes + approval records into a versioned JSON envelope with `run_id`, `policy_version`, `created_at`. Persist violations/approvals in the same durable `SessionStore` as conversation (agent-framework already does for `ToolApprovalState`; extend to `audit_log`). Add `approved_at`/`approved_by`/`approvalFingerprint` on state transition `AWAITING_APPROVAL -> RUNNING` instead of inferring from next event.

- If operating a **policy decision point sidecar** (OPA model): treat OPA as one evidence *source*, not the system. Correlate via `decision_id`/`trace_id` (`v1/server/server.go:1115-1116`, `v1/logging/logging.go:274-283`), archive bundles by `revision` (`v1/plugins/logs/plugin.go:79-81`) externally, and deploy a collector that adds disk WAL / acknowledgment before dropping bounded buffers. Build a session aggregator `GET /v1/report?decision_id=` if "run" spans multiple decisions.

- If operating a **SaaS observability/governance platform** (Langfuse model): centralize mutation audit with `before`/`after` and deterministic export (strongest pattern here), but also emit a `policyDecision` record on every `throwIfNoProjectAccess`/`throwIfNoEntitlement` deny, add an approval ledger with `approvedAt`/`approvedBy`, make `audit_logs` append-only (`REVOKE UPDATE` + hash chain or per-export HMAC per `webhooks.ts:535`), and add `auditLogRetentionDays` + `deleteAuditLogsOlderThanDays` with legal-hold toggle. Without the join, evidence stays in four stores.

- If owning only the **frontend** (OpenHands model): do not claim governance — add a server-owned `GET /v1/{automationId}/runs/{runId}/evidence` endpoint returning a signed bundle (run row + `trigger`/`filter` eval + trajectory hash + `usable_tools` + `agent_settings`) and render it; stop client-assembled pagination (`automation-activity-log-export.ts:121-135` drifts on concurrent append). Any DefenseClaw/SIEM claim must be backed by a mock E2E that asserts a decision lands in the audit store and forwards via a fake OTLP collector.

**Rule of thumb for retention:** For observability data, Langfuse's re-read + `skip if null/0` + cutoff pattern is the model to copy. For audit evidence, enforce *either* indefinite with WORM/Object Lock *or* explicit TTL with tombstone — never unbounded + mutable, never volatile FIFO.

## Practical Tips

Patterns to copy now:

- Audit every mutating handler through a single `auditLog()` writer with `before`/`after` snapshots, actor + role-at-time, and `No FK` preservation — Langfuse `auditLog.ts:86-155` + `schema.prisma:852-876` is the reference. Enforce via lint ("every mutation must call `auditLog` or declare `noAudit` exemption").
- Use OTLP/HTTP pluggable export as transport, but commit to a SIEM field mapping (ECS/OCSF) and ship *one* reference exporter (file or OTLP) with an integration test — don't leave mapping to every deployer.
- Version your checkpoint schema (`RunState` `SCHEMA_VERSION_SUMMARIES` 1.0–1.17, `run_state.py:182-218`) and / or total-order events by `sequenceNumber` / `orderBy createdAt desc` for deterministic re-export.
- Re-read policy config inside the retention job before deleting (Langfuse `handleDataRetentionProcessingJob.ts:26-56`) — prevents a retention-disable race from becoming data loss.
- Harden externalization: HMAC header (`webhooks.ts:535-541`), `validateWebhookURL` + whitelist, redirect-chain logging, truncation at `responseBody.substring(0,1000)` awareness.

Patterns to avoid or delay:

- Don't store governance violations only in a process-local `list[dict]` (`security.py:1698`) — you will lose them on crash with no WAL. If a library cannot own storage, at least emit them as OTel span events / log records so the downstream collector owns durability.
- Don't drop evidence on bounded queues without a dead-letter or `EvidenceDroppedError` surfaced in `RunResult`. `BatchTraceProcessor` warning-only drops (`tracing/processors.py:603`) violate governance invariants.
- Don't entitle-gate the only read path for audit logs (`auditLogs.ts:87-92` `throwIfNoEntitlement("audit-logs")`) if compliance mandates universal audit — store evidence but gate the UI, not the export pipeline.
- Don't ship `DONT_LOG_*` redaction without a compensating field (`truncated: true`, `redacted: true`) so auditors can distinguish "no data" from "elided".

Caution signs the system is becoming brittle:

- `audit_log` growth without `max_entries`/TTL or file rotation beyond dev logs (`logger.mjs:66`); traces show token counts but no tool args by default (`observability.py:928-934`).
- `AllByOrg` vs `all` dual scopes diverge (`auditLogs.ts:78-277`); `useEventStore` dedup hides late-arriving events (`use-event-store.ts:24-35`); export paging uses offset refreshed from `total` (`automation-activity-log-export.ts:121-135`) — all hint at concurrent-mutation drift.
- `nd_builtin_cache` stripped on oversize (`encoder.go:108-131`); `updatedAt` auto-managed on audit rows; approval `expiresAt` without `approvedAt` — evidence mutability or incompleteness.
- Metrics for drops (`decision_logs_dropped_*`) exist but no status API exposes them to the SIEM consuming logs.

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **In-memory governance ledger:** `PolicyEnforcementFunctionMiddleware.audit_log: list[dict]` (`security.py:1698`, `2174-2184`) with manual `clear()`, no TTL, no persistence, no timestamps — evidence vanishes on restart. Symptom: only one audit test (`test_security.py:613-616` asserts length 0).
- **Asymmetric durability:** `ToolApprovalState` in `AgentSession.state` (`_tool_approval.py:248-275`) survives restarts while the violation that triggered it does not — forensic link broken.
- **Tracer-as-audit overreach:** Governance decisions (`max_allowed_confidentiality` violation) live only in Python dicts, not spans, so SIEM viewers see tool execution but not *why blocked* (`observability.py` has no policy-decision span attribute).
- **Silent non-governance retention:** `TRANSPARENCY_FAQ.md:119-121` / `README.md:216` delegating retention to deployer with no defaults — caller can pick indefinite or `max_messages=0` (zero retention, `redis/tests/test_providers.py:503`).
- **Mutable, unsigned audit rows:** `audit_logs.before/after: String?` (`schema.prisma:866-867`), `updatedAt` writable, no hash chain / `REVOKE UPDATE` — compromised DB principal can rewrite history.
- **Deny without record:** `throwIfNoProjectAccess` / `throwIfNoEntitlement` (`auditLogs.ts:87-99` etc.) throw `TRPCError` before audit write; failed exfiltration probes vanish except in HTTP logs.
- **Volatile bounded buffer as evidence store:** OPA `logBuffer` FIFO drops oldest (`buffer.go:32-57`), SDK `queue.Full` drops spans (`tracing/processors.py:603-604`). No spool, no WORM, `Stop` may leave buffer non-empty (`plugin.go:665-713`).
- **Client-side pagination for authoritative exports:** `fetchAllActivityLogExportRows` paging in the browser (`automation-activity-log-export.ts:121-135`) plus cloud caps (`event-service.api.ts:120` limit 100) cause truncation or duplication under concurrent mutation.
- **Redaction without signal:** `DONT_LOG_MODEL_DATA` prints `"Trace/Span data is redacted"` (`tracing/processors.py:32`); `validateWebhookURL`/`responseBody.substring(0,1000)` truncates — audit sees lossy evidence with no `truncated` flag.
