## Executive Summary

No source in this cohort ships a first-class quality-cost router that automatically routes cheap models to simple requests and expensive models to complex ones. Six of seven sources score **2/10 (Absent)**; one — pydantic-ai — scores **5/10 (Present but inconsistent)** on the strength of a mature failover primitive, not on cost-aware routing. The unanimous pattern is **delegated manual tiering**: every harness exposes a broad catalog of models as independently addressable single-model clients (`BaseChatClient` in agent-framework `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_clients.py:416`, `LLM(model=...)` factory in crewai `lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:393`, `ModelParams` in langfuse `packages/shared/src/server/llm/types.ts:272`, `BaseChatModel` injection in langgraph `libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:279`, `Agent.model` in openai-agents-sdk `src/agents/agent.py:337`, `LLM profile` in openhands `src/api/profiles-service/profiles-service.api.ts:91`, `KnownModelName` in pydantic-ai `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/_known_model_names.py:13`) and leaves cheap-vs-expensive selection to application code, LLM-semantic handoff, or human click.

Two product-shape outliers are absences by design: langfuse is an observability platform that invests deeply in **post-hoc pricing-tier accounting** (`packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:116`) rather than live routing, and openhands is a thin frontend whose LLM execution lives in a sibling `software-agent-sdk` (`AGENTS.md:31-36`). The remaining agent harnesses converge on the same extension seams — middleware/callable/capability wrappers — but none supply built-in criteria (cost, latency, quality, risk), policy config, fallback chains across cost tiers, or routing-decision traces.

The only durable fallback chain found is pydantic-ai's `FallbackModel` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:86`), a sequential exception- and response-triggered failover with streaming, continuation pinning, and OTel patching, tested across 3,300+ lines (`tests/models/test_fallback.py:1`). Even it is **reliability-first, not economy-first** — it retries on failure, not on cost/latency/quality signals. The immediate lesson: if you need cheap→expensive escalation today, you must build it yourself on top of `ChatMiddleware` / `ModelSelector` / dynamic callable / `with_fallbacks`, and you must add the observability and policy layer that no harness provides.

## Core Thesis

**Quality-cost routing does not exist as a framework primitive in the current agent-harness generation; it exists only as a user-owned composition pattern on top of single-model clients.** The studied harnesses deliberately optimize for provider fidelity, graph flexibility, or observability determinism, and they push the hard parts — classifier, budget, SLO, tier registry, fallback chain, and decision trace — to the deployer. Convergence is therefore negative: all sources agree to *not* route, while divergence is explained by *why* they don't route and *what hook* they offer instead. That makes the dimension's failure modes systemic (no graceful degradation on frontier overload, no budget enforcement, no attribution of why a tier was chosen) and the remedy architectural: introduce an explicit `RoutingPolicy` + `Tier` abstraction with first-class fallback chains and `routing.decision` spans, rather than bolting heuristics onto retry or semantic handoff.

## Approach Models

Three approach models emerge when clustered by *why* routing is absent and *what mechanism* is offered instead. They are not quality rankings — they are valid responses to different constraints.

### Model A: Delegated Manual Tiering (agent-framework, crewai, openai-agents-sdk)

**Shape:** Single-model-per-instance with explicit caller-owned assignment. Each client/agent holds exactly one `model` string; tiering is achieved by instantiating N clients with cheap/expensive ids and wiring them manually.

*   **agent-framework:** `Agent(client: SupportsChatGetResponse)` + `default_options["model"]` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:813`), per-run override via `_merge_options` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:138`), provider fidelity via verbatim `ChatOptions` forwarding (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:619`). Routing seam is `ChatMiddleware`/`AgentMiddleware` layering (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_clients.py:984`).
*   **crewai:** `LLM.__new__` factory dispatching by provider prefix to native completion class or LiteLLM fallback (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:393`) with per-role slots `Agent.llm` / `Crew.manager_llm` / `Crew.planning_llm` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/agent/core.py:223`, `lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:275`). LiteLLM is used as completion backend, never as `litellm.Router`.
*   **openai-agents-sdk:** `Agent.model` (`src/agents/agent.py:337`) + `RunConfig.model` override (`src/agents/run_config.py:353`) + `MultiProvider` prefix multiplexing (`litellm/`, `any-llm/`, `openai/` at `src/agents/models/multi_provider.py:62`) + LLM-semantic handoff (`src/agents/handoffs/__init__.py:125`). `ModelSettings.extra_args.service_tier` (`src/agents/model_settings.py:183`) exposes OpenAI cost tier but only as caller-set passthrough.

**Why it exists:** Maximizes provider fidelity and debuggability; avoids non-determinism, classifier latency, and double-invocation cost of auto-routing. Fits library-vs-application boundary where the harness is a toolkit, not a gateway.

### Model B: Orchestration-Delegated Callable / Graph-Node Routing (langgraph, pydantic-ai selector half, agent-framework workflows)

**Shape:** No model-tier type; the graph/node is the router. User authors a classifier node or supplies a per-step callable that returns a model.

*   **langgraph:** `create_react_agent(model=Callable[[State, Runtime[Context]], BaseChatModel])` (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:279`) resolved each `call_model` via `_resolve_model` (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:599`). Resilience is generic `RetryPolicy`/`TimeoutPolicy`/`error_handler` fallback node (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:418`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/state.py:298`), all node-execution concerns, not tier concerns. Graph routing (`add_conditional_edges`/`BranchSpec`/`Send` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/state.py:982`) is topology routing, not model routing.
*   **pydantic-ai (selector half):** `SelectModel(ModelSelector)` capability (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/capabilities/select_model.py:11`) with `ModelSelectionContext{deps, messages, usage, run_step, model}` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/__init__.py:350`) evaluated per step in agent graph (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_agent_graph.py:392`). Last non-None `get_model()` wins.

**Why it exists:** Keeps harness model-agnostic and maximally flexible; reuses existing workflow/tooling primitives for routing. Fits orchestration frameworks where any routing policy can be expressed as a node + conditional edge, at the cost of zero out-of-box cost optimization.

### Model C: Scope-Delegated Absence + Accounting Substitute (langfuse, openhands, pydantic-ai fallback half)

**Shape:** Routing is intentionally absent because product scope delegates it elsewhere or substitutes post-hoc accounting.

*   **langfuse:** Observability/evaluation platform; runtime selection is one static `InAppAgentModelConfig{modelId, titleModelId}` resolved from `LANGFUSE_AWS_BEDROCK_MODEL` env (`packages/shared/src/in-app-agent/server/modelProvider.ts:4`). Live routing is absent. Instead, `matchPricingTier` (`packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:116`) implements priority-ordered tier matching (default + conditional regex on `usage_details` + exact `model_parameters`/`metadata` match) to compute `modelPrices` → `calculateUsageCosts` per observation (`worker/src/services/IngestionService/index.ts:1341`) and persist `usage_pricing_tier_id/name` to ClickHouse (`packages/shared/clickhouse/migrations/unclustered/0031_add_usage_pricing_tier_columns.up.sql:1`). The `modelMatchLocalCache → Redis → Postgres` lookup cascade (`packages/shared/src/server/ingestion/modelMatch.ts:58`) is metadata caching, not LLM fallback.
*   **openhands:** Frontend-only; backend owns LLM execution. Frontend holds single `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.llm_model = "openhands/kimi-k3"` (`src/services/settings.ts:6`) and a flat `ProfilesService.listProfiles` catalog (`src/api/profiles-service/profiles-service.api.ts:91`). Switch is single-shot `switchProfile`/`switchAcpModel` RPC (`src/api/conversation-service/agent-server-conversation-service.api.ts:891`) with toast on error — no chain, no retry. `max_budget_per_task` (`src/types/settings.ts:143`) halts, never downgrades.
*   **pydantic-ai (fallback half):** The exception to the cohort: `FallbackModel(Model)` with `models: list[Model]` + `fallback_on: FallbackOn` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:86`) provides sequential failover with predicate auto-detection (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:135`), streaming fallback with `AsyncExitStack` (`fallback.py:320`), continuation pinning via `metadata['__pydantic_ai__']['fallback_model_id']` (`fallback.py:461`), and `FallbackExceptionGroup` aggregation (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/exceptions.py:612`). Best-effort cost via `genai-prices` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_cost.py:98`) and `UsageLimits.cost_limit` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:417`) exists but is perimeter, not routing.

**Why it exists:** Langfuse and openhands are not agent harnesses in the routing sense (observability UI vs backend-owned execution), so absence is intentional isolation. Pydantic-ai's failover is the only case where a true fallback chain was built, and its investment (55+ tests at `tests/models/test_fallback.py:1`, covering pinning, locks, cancellation, rejected-cost roll-up) shows what "routing" costs to ship correctly — even though the fallback is reliability-triggered, not cost-triggered.

## Pattern Catalog

### 1. Single-Model-Per-Instance with Verbatim Passthrough

What problem it solves: Preserves provider fidelity and keeps framework surface minimal as providers evolve new fields (`reasoning.effort`, `service_tier`, `verbosity`).

Sources demonstrating it: agent-framework (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:813`, `python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:279`), crewai (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:368`, `lib/crewai/src/crewai/llms/base_llm.py:169`), langgraph (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:278`), openai-agents-sdk (`src/agents/agent.py:337`, `src/agents/models/default_models.py:99`), openhands (`src/services/settings.ts:6`), pydantic-ai (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/agent/__init__.py:751`), langfuse (`packages/shared/src/server/llm/llmText.ts:168`).

Why it works: Construction-time binding is explicit, grep-able, and debuggable; `validate_chat_options` / `_prepare_options` style forwarding avoids framework coupling to provider pricing or model evolution.

When to copy: When building a library/SDK that must stay provider-agnostic and not own cost policy.

When overkill/risky: When you own the gateway and mixed workloads would save 30–50% with cheap-first routing — static assignment then forces duplication of agent definitions and manual per-role bookkeeping (crewai `Crew.manager_llm` etc.).

Evidence: agent-framework `python/packages/core/agent_framework/__init__.py:43` (many independent clients, no aggregator); openai-agents-sdk `src/agents/model_settings.py:88` (no `cost_threshold` field).

### 2. Middleware / Capability / Callable Extension Seam as DIY Router

What problem it solves: Lets users implement quality-cost routing without the framework shipping policy.

Sources: agent-framework `ChatMiddleware`/`AgentMiddleware` layering (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_clients.py:984`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_middleware.py:1744`), langgraph dynamic model callable (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:599`), pydantic-ai `SelectModel`/`ModelSelector` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/capabilities/select_model.py:11`, `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/capabilities/abstract.py:64`), implicitly crewai `create_llm` normalization (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/utilities/llm_utils.py:13`) where callers hold the reference.

Why it works: Leverages existing interception points; typing (`Runtime[ContextT]`, `ModelSelectionContext`) makes context-aware selection possible (tenant, `state`, `run_step`).

When to copy: When you need tenant- or request-specific model choice (multi-tenant `runtime.context.model_name` example in langgraph) and can accept owning tracing, caching, and policy validation.

When risky: The burden is entirely on the user — no validation, serialization, or operator tunability. Multiple sources note naming collisions if a future router is called `Router` (crewai `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/dsl/_router.py:97` vs future cost router).

### 3. Sequential Failover Chain with Predicate Handlers (the only mature fallback)

What problem it solves: Graceful degradation on transient provider failure without user-written retry loops.

Sources: pydantic-ai `FallbackModel` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:86-554`) — sole source with first-class implementation. Partial shadows: openai-agents-sdk `ModelRetrySettings` + `retry_policies.any/all` (`src/agents/retry.py:304`), langgraph `RetryPolicy` + `error_handler` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:418`), crewai `Agent.max_retry_limit` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/agent/core.py:255`). All three shadows retry the *same* model, not a tier chain.

Why it works: `FallbackOn = Sequence[ExceptionType|ExceptionHandler|ResponseHandler]` (`fallback.py:50`) auto-detected via `ModelResponse` type hint (`fallback.py:67`), per-model `prepare_request`/`prepare_messages` re-preparation (`fallback.py:284`), `FallbackExceptionGroup` aggregation (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/exceptions.py:612`), and continuation pinning (`fallback.py:461`) together handle streaming, suspension, and provider-profile differences.

When to copy: When reliability requires ordered degradation (primary → cheaper → fail-open), especially for background/suspended turns.

When overkill: When fallback is driven by quality score rather than exception — `FallbackModel` conflates error handling with quality gating (`fallback.py:50-77`), and an empty `fallback_on` raises `UserError` (`fallback.py:160`) while a never-raised error type silently never falls back.

Evidence: 55+ dedicated tests (`tests/models/test_fallback.py:1`) including reentrant locks (`tests/models/test_fallback.py:2013`), continuation rewind (`tests/models/test_fallback.py:2164`), rejected-cost aggregation (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:296`).

### 4. Post-Hoc Cost/Usage Accounting Without Routing Feedback

What problem it solves: Budget visibility, cost attribution, and billing correctness even when live routing is absent.

Sources: langfuse `matchPricingTier` → `calculateUsageCosts` (`worker/src/services/IngestionService/index.ts:1341`, `packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:116`) persisting `usage_pricing_tier_id/name` (`packages/shared/clickhouse/migrations/unclustered/0031_add_usage_pricing_tier_columns.up.sql:1`); crewai `UsageMetrics` normalization (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/types/usage_metrics.py:32`) with `delta_since` (`usage_metrics.py:79`) and `completion_cost` field (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:370`) stored but never acted upon; pydantic-ai `genai-prices` snapshot (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_cost.py:98`) + `RequestUsage.cost: Decimal` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:130`); openai-agents-sdk `GenerationSpanData{model, usage}` (`src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:169`) + `Usage` (`src/agents/usage.py`); agent-framework OTel `gen_ai.request.model`/`input_tokens`+ histograms (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:249`); openhands `accumulated_cost` (`src/stores/metrics-store.ts:4`) + `BudgetDisplay` (`src/components/features/conversation-panel/budget-display.tsx:12`).

Why it works: Decouples deterministic accounting from non-deterministic routing; single-default-tier invariant (`packages/shared/src/features/model-pricing/validation.ts:149`) guarantees cost never fails silently.

When risky: Creates illusion of cost control without enforcement — `UsageMetrics` is reporting not guarding; `max_budget_per_task` (`src/types/settings.ts:143`) halts but never downgrades; `cost: Decimal | None` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:130`) may be `None` when pricing data missing, so selector never triggers.

### 5. Semantic / Agent-Level Handoff as Coarse Cost Proxy

What problem it solves: Cheap triage agent → expensive specialist handoff without a per-token router.

Sources: openai-agents-sdk `triage_agent → french/spanish/english_agent` (`examples/agent_patterns/routing.py:30`, `src/agents/handoffs/__init__.py:125`) and `MultiProvider` prefix routing; agent-framework `HandoffOrchestrator`/`GroupChatOrchestrator` + `SwitchCaseEdgeGroup`/`FanOutEdgeGroup` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:296`); langgraph manual `router_node` test pattern (`libs/langgraph/tests/test_large_cases.py:6481`); crewai hierarchical `Crew.manager_llm` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:275`).

Why it works: Reuses agentic orchestration primitives already present; decision is delegated to an LLM classifier tool rather than a framework policy.

When risky: LLM may mis-route simple requests to expensive specialists with no cost circuit-breaker (agent-framework warns `service_tier: flex` trades cost for latency at `python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:254`; openai-agents-sdk lacks any `quality_gate`).

### 6. Priority-Ordered First-Match with Default-Tier Fallback (billing pattern reusable for routing)

What problem it solves: Deterministic fallback when no conditional tier matches.

Sources: langfuse pricing-tier system — `validatePricingTiers` requires exactly one `isDefault:true` with `priority:0` and empty `conditions` (`packages/shared/src/features/model-pricing/validation.ts:174`), `matchPricingTier` filters non-default by `priority ASC`, tests `evaluateConditions` (AND), returns first match else default (`packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:122`). Reused identically in ingestion, cost preview (`web/src/server/api/routers/models.ts:449`), and tests (`worker/src/__tests__/pricing-tier-matcher.test.ts:659`).

Why it works: Guarantees never-fails invariant; throttling tests and regex thresholds (`pricing-tier-matcher.test.ts:659-1582`) prove threshold brittleness is manageable for billing.

When to copy: Directly for a future `RoutingPolicy.tiers: [{model, priority, when: "input_tokens < 800 && no_tools"}]` with default cheapest tier.

Evidence: `fern/apis/server/definition/commons.yml:775` requiring exactly one default tier; `packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:139` fallback return.

### 7. Budget-as-Hard-Cap Instead of Budget-as-Router Input

What problem it solves: Prevents runaway cost without building a router.

Sources: openhands `max_budget_per_task: number | null` (`src/types/settings.ts:143`) rendered by `BudgetProgressBar` (`src/components/features/conversation-panel/budget-progress-bar.tsx:10`); pydantic-ai `UsageLimits.cost_limit: Decimal` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:417`) with `check_cost()` warning `CostNotFoundWarning` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:528`); agent-framework note that compaction at invocation limits strips local tools (`python/packages/core/AGENTS.md` tool-approval section) without downgrading model.

When to copy: As safety net; complement, not substitute for routing.

When risky: Exhaustion has no degrade path — conversation stalls until user intervenes.

## Key Differences

**Breadth vs routing.** crewai offers the broadest catalog (14 native providers `lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:327`, 100+ `LLM_CONTEXT_WINDOW_SIZES` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:168`, `constants.MODELS` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/constants.py:137`) yet zero tiering — breadth is catalog, not policy. pydantic-ai has a smaller `KnownModelName` literal union (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/_known_model_names.py:13`) but is the only source where breadth is wired to failover (`FallbackModel`). Langfuse also has wide catalog (`packages/shared/src/server/llm/types.ts:315` ~75 OpenAI + 25 Anthropic ids) but only for playground selection, not routing.

**Retry vs fallback.** All harnesses have retry; only one has tier fallback. openai-agents-sdk `ModelRetrySettings` + `retry_policies` (`src/agents/retry.py:210`) and langgraph `RetryPolicy(max_attempts, backoff_factor, retry_on)` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:418`) retry the *same* model with replay-safety vetoes (`src/agents/run_internal/model_retry.py:389` `is_abort`/`replay_safety`). crewai `max_retry_limit=2` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/agent/core.py:255`) similarly retries same agent. pydantic-ai alone iterates an ordered `models: list[Model]` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:229`) and treats response handlers as first-class; `RetryPolicy` storm on quota errors (langgraph's `initial_interval=0.5` with jitter at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:424`) increases cost before any escalation.

**Accounting richness vs actionability.** Every source instruments cost/usage post-hoc but none feeds it back. Langfuse is richest (`UsageCondition` regex thresholds + `AttributeCondition` exact match dual-source at `packages/shared/src/features/model-pricing/validation.ts:60`, priority-ordered first-match) yet the data lives in ClickHouse observations and never influences next invocation. crewai normalizes `prompt_tokens/completion_tokens/cached/reasoning_tokens` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/types/usage_metrics.py:112`) and tracks per-crew aggregation (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:2201`) but `completion_cost` is never populated for routing (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:370`). pydantic-ai comes closest by exposing `usage.cost` to the `ModelSelector` context (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/__init__.py:350`) and by aggregating `rejected_cost` into the winning response (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:296`), yet no built-in predicate compares it to a budget.

**Semantic handoff vs cost routing.** openai-agents-sdk and agent-framework both have first-class handoff/orchestrator primitives (`Handoff` at `src/agents/handoffs/__init__.py:125`, `HandoffOrchestrator`/`GroupChatOrchestrator` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_agent.py:598`) that look like routing but are capability-based delegation (language, specialty), not cost-based. **Provider routing vs quality routing.** openai-agents-sdk `MultiProvider` (`src/agents/models/multi_provider.py:62`) and crewai's `LLM.__new__` provider dispatch (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:393`) both route by string prefix (`openai/`, `litellm/`, `anthropic/`) to solve auth/endpoint multiplexing. ## Tradeoffs

**Simplicity and determinism vs autonomous cost optimization.**
Benefit: Single-model-per-instance is trivial to reason about, audit, and snapshot; no classifier latency or double-invocation. Cost: Forfeits 30–50% savings on mixed workloads; duplication of agent definitions is manual. Best fit: libraries/SDKs that must stay provider-agnostic and release-decoupled (agent-framework, crewai, langgraph). Failure mode: Silent cost drift — e.g., openai-agents-sdk `RunConfig.model` globally overrides per-agent tiers (`src/agents/run_config.py:353`) with only `generation` model field as signal. Alternative: pydantic-ai's `SelectModel` with `ModelSelectionContext.usage` offers per-step economy at the cost of user-owned heuristics.

**Provider fidelity vs portable cost model.**
Benefit: Forwarding `ChatOptions` verbatim (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_clients.py:329`, `python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:619`) preserves access to every new provider field without a framework release. Cost: Framework cannot reason portably about "flex is cheaper" across Anthropic/Bedrock; a provider-agnostic price table is absent (agent-framework notes no `model_id → $/input,$/output` map). Best fit: frameworks supporting many providers (agent-framework 6+ provider packages). Failure mode: `service_tier: flex` increases latency without SLO guard (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:254`); typo in `extra_args.service_tier` silently passes validation (`src/agents/model_settings.py:333`). Alternative: OpenRouter `sort: price` delegates correctness to gateway.

**Graph-level routing flexibility vs tier-specific safeguards.**
Benefit: `add_conditional_edges` + dynamic callable supports any policy as a node/classifier. Cost: Zero built-in safeguards — no threshold, no deduplication, no circuit breaker. Best fit: orchestration-first teams that already own graph topology. Failure mode: Retry storm on quota (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:424` `initial_interval=0.5` * 3 attempts) before any escalation; timeout vs retry gap (`sync_timeout_unsupported` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_retry.py:583`) makes latency-driven fallback async-only. Alternative: `FallbackModel` sequential fallback with `FallbackOn` predicates.

**Billing determinism vs execution resilience.**
Benefit: Langfuse's default-tier-always-matches (`packages/shared/src/features/model-pricing/validation.ts:149`, `packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:139`) and per-row persistence make costs auditable and retroactively repriceable; cache tiering `LocalCache (TTL 10s) → Redis → Postgres` (`packages/shared/src/server/ingestion/modelMatch.ts:58`) is durable. Cost: Provides no resilience — `executeInAppAgentRun` fails the run (`AGENT_ERROR`/`INIT_FAILED` at `worker/src/features/in-app-agent/executeInAppAgentRun.ts:203`) rather than hedging to a cheaper model. Failure mode: Mis-matched `model` regex yields `{model:null, pricingTiers:[]}` (`packages/shared/src/server/ingestion/modelMatch.ts:272`) and `null` tier, silently under-reporting spend. Alternative: Wire pricing-tier thresholds as router inputs, not just post-hoc tags.

**Retry conservatism vs degraded availability.**
Benefit: openai-agents-sdk vetoes `is_abort`/`emitted_retry_unsafe_event`/`replay_unsafe_request`/`stateful_request` (`src/agents/run_internal/model_retry.py:389`, `COMPATIBILITY_CONVERSATION_LOCKED_RETRIES=3` at `run_internal/model_retry.py:50`) prevents duplicate side-effects for `previous_response_id` chains. Cost: Transient frontier overload does not automatically degrade to `gpt-5-mini`, leaving throughput unused. Failure mode: Stateful pre-check that set context then fails cannot even retry cheap model — forces full chain restart.

**Framework-owned catalog breadth vs tier abstraction.**
Benefit: Catalog breadth (crewai 14 providers, langfuse 75+ OpenAI models) maximizes choice. Cost: Without tier labels, users must maintain private mappings of cheap/fast vs capable models, and validation is pattern-based not semantic (`_matches_provider_pattern` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:515`, `validate_chat_options` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:3136`). Failure mode: Provider SDK missing raises hard `ImportError` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:491`) with no cheaper fallback attempt.

**Observability of model identity vs observability of routing rationale.**
Benefit: Emitting `gen_ai.request.model`/`gen_ai.response.model` + usage is sufficient for post-hoc dashboards. Cost: Cannot attribute cost spike to routing ("routed to mini because input_tokens < 512"); incident response cannot answer "why was gpt-4o chosen over mini". Failure mode: Azure `x-ms-served-model` divergence (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:819`) — cost attribution on requested alias misattributes. Alternative: `routing.decision` span event `{chosen_model, candidates, reason, cost_estimate, fallback_attempt}` along one framework now hints at.

## Decision Guide

**Use Delegated Manual Tiering (Model A) when:**
- You are building a library/SDK, not a gateway, and provider evolution is high (new fields, new models monthly).
- Determinism and auditability outweigh cost entropy (regulated workloads, eval harnesses).
- Your workload is homogeneous (all hard reasoning, or all trivial classification) — the 30% savings from tiering doesn't justify classifier complexity.
Decision rule: If you can name every agent's model at authorship time and the set fits in a YAML file, keep manual assignment and invest in `UsageMetrics` dashboards first.

**Use Orchestration-Delegated Callable/Graph Routing (Model B) when:**
- Routing depends on request context, conversation history, or retrieval quality (multi-tenant, complex branching, phase-aware tasks like plan → retrieve → synthesize).
- You already use LangGraph/pydantic-graph style graphs — classifier node + conditional edge is natural.
- You can own policy validation, serialization, and tracing.
Decision rule: If routing predicate needs `state`/`deps`/`messages`/`usage` together, use `SelectModel`/`Callable[[State, Runtime]]` and emit a custom `routing.decision` event yourself; if predicate is purely failure-driven, prefer Model C.

**Use Failover Chain (pydantic-ai Model C) when:**
- Resilience, not economy, is the driver (provider outages, 429/503, content-filter fallback).
- Suspended/background turns require model affinity (continuation pinning) and you need `FallbackExceptionGroup` diagnostics.
- Streaming and cancellation semantics matter.
Decision rule: If ordered degradation is `primary → degraded → fail-open` with predicates on exception type/status/response, copy `FallbackModel.fallback_on: FallbackOn` pattern. Add rejected-cost aggregation if spend tracking spans the chain (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:296`).

**Use Scope-Delegated + Accounting (Langs) when:**
- Your product is observability/evaluation or thin frontend, not execution gateway — delegate routing to the backend/gateway that owns credentials and retries.
- Post-hoc cost correctness is the priority (audits, re-pricing, per-project billing).
Decision rule: If you pick this, close the loop by exposing tier metadata in the catalog UI (`ProfileInfo` with `tier`/`cost_per_1k` at `src/api/profiles-service/profiles-service.api.ts:50`) and by persisting `routing_decision {attempts[], chosenModel, reason}` alongside `usage_pricing_tier_*` in ClickHouse — otherwise accounting stays decoupled from execution.

**When to mix:** Most production systems should combine Model A for static roles (planner = expensive, summarizer = cheap) with Model C for resilience (primary → fallback on 429) and Model B for the one adaptive step (classifier → cheap/fast vs expensive). Build order: 1) failover chain (reliability), 2) graph-node cheap classifier (economy), 3) declarative routing policy (operability).

**When to avoid any router:** Budgets are already capped by `max_iterations`/`UsageLimits.cost_limit`, workload is low-volume, or provider pricing variance <2× — the operational overhead of a router exceeds savings.

## Practical Tips

**Patterns to copy:**
- **Explicit single-model clients with per-run override isolation.** Use `_merge_options` style stripping of `None` and copy-on-write (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:138`) so transient `model: "gpt-4o-mini"` does not leak across turns.
- **Sequential failover with typed predicate union.** Copy `FallbackOn = ExceptionType | ExceptionHandler | ResponseHandler` auto-detected via `ModelResponse` hint (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:50-77`), with `prepare_request` per inner model (`fallback.py:284`), `AsyncExitStack` for streams (`fallback.py:320`), and `FallbackExceptionGroup` aggregation (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/exceptions.py:612`). This is the only proven chain shape in the cohort.
- **Continuation pinning via response metadata.** Stamp chosen `model_id` into `metadata['__pydantic_ai__']['fallback_model_id']` (`fallback.py:461`) and honour it on replay with rewind (`fallback.py:522`), to avoid re-issuing suspended turns against the wrong model.
- **Priority-ordered first-match with mandatory default.** Enforce exactly one `isDefault:true` tier (`packages/shared/src/features/model-pricing/validation.ts:174`) and iterate `priority ASC` (`packages/shared/src/server/pricing-tiers/matcher.ts:122`) — guarantees never-fails fast path and applies directly to routing tiers.
- **Provider-prefix multiplexing for heterogeneous routing.** Use `MultiProvider` prefix scheme (`src/agents/models/multi_provider.py:62`) to author `litellm/` or `any-llm/` cheap tiers alongside `openai/` frontier tiers without alias collisions; validate `service_tier` passthrough (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:254`).
- **Per-step context object for selectors.** Expose `ModelSelectionContext{deps, messages, usage, run_step}` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/__init__.py:350`) so selectors can threshold on `input_tokens`, `cost`, or retrieval hit without framework heuristics.
- **Delta-since usage snapshots.** Offer `UsageMetrics.delta_since(baseline)` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/types/usage_metrics.py:79`) as primitive for cost accounting per call, even before a router consumes it.

**Patterns to avoid or delay:**
- Don't build a router on top of same-model retry (`ModelRetrySettings`, `RetryPolicy`, `max_retry_limit`). Retry retries the expensive frontier; it wastes latency before escalation and masks quota exhaustion as success.
- Don't use LLM-semantic handoff as a cost proxy beyond one triage step. Handoff relies on LLM tool-choice non-determinism and has no cost circuit-breaker — simple requests can be mis-routed to expensive specialists.
- Don't multiplex via OpenRouter `sort: price` alone if you also run self-hosted or multi-provider workloads — it creates a split-brain where gateway users get routing and others don't (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/openrouter.py:195`).
- Don't name a future cost router `Router` in CrewAI scope — `Flow @router` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/dsl/_router.py:97`) and `FlowConversationalRouterDefinition` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/conversational_definition.py:15`) already collide.
- Don't mutate a shared `options` dict to swap models — agent-framework warns `continuation_token` reuse can leak cheap-model choice into next iteration (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:778`).

**Decision rules for choosing:**
- If SLO is p95 latency, bound `service_tier: flex` or cheap-model use to non-latency-critical intents; otherwise stick to predictable tier.
- If `genai-prices` snapshot lacks pricing for your model, treat `cost` as `None` and fail safe to configured default, not to cheapest — `CostNotFoundWarning` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/usage.py:528`) is the signal.
- If every turn uses the same provider, prefer `SelectModel` over a gateway; if providers vary, prefer `MultiProvider` + gateway knobs.

**Caution signs that routing is becoming brittle:**
- Dashboards show `gen_ai.request.model` changing but no `routing.decision` rationale — operability already degraded; add a span event before adding more tiers.
- `max_budget_per_task` or `cost_limit` fires but workload continues on expensive model until halt — budget is halt-only, not routing input; wire `usage.cost` into selector or fallback predicate.
- `x-ms-served-model` diverges from requested `model` alias — attribution is broken; router must read `ChatResponse.model`, not `options["model"]`.
- Rejected-stream costs undercount — pydantic-ai `request_stream` does not roll up `rejected_cost` (`fallback.py:296` path is `request` only) — caller must sum from `RunUsage`.

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **Routing by retry.** Treating `ModelRetrySettings`/`RetryPolicy`/`Agent.max_retry_limit` as a fallback chain. Symptom: 3× retries on 429 with exponential backoff (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:424`) burn latency and cost before any cheaper tier is tried; openai-agents-sdk vetoes actually tighten the trap. Fix: separate transient-retry (same model, 1–2 hops) from tier-fallback (ordered model list, max 2 hops, tagged `generation retry_reason`).
- **Tracing identity, not decision.** Recording only `gen_ai.request.model`/`response.model`/`usage` without candidates, scores, or reason. Symptom: cannot answer "why was cheap not chosen?" or "cheap hit-rate dropped 30%". Evidence spans every harness: agent-framework `observability.py:290` (no `routing.decision`), crewai `llm_events.py:38` (no `RoutingDecision`), langgraph `types.py:122` (graph routing only), openai-agents-sdk `span_data.py:169` (generation only).
- **Budget as display.** Accumulating `accumulated_cost`/`UsageMetrics`/`RequestUsage.cost` without enforcement. Symptom: dashboards look green while drift burns budget; openhands `BudgetDisplay` (`src/components/features/conversation-panel/budget-display.tsx:22`) and crewai `completion_cost` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:370`) are unused for selection. Fix: gate `UsageLimits.cost_limit`/`max_budget_per_task` into the selector or force downgrade mid-trajectory.
- **Semantic handoff drift.** LLM triage routing (openhands isn't, but openai-agents-sdk `examples/agent_patterns/routing.py:30`) silently sending simple requests to specialists. Symptom: cost variance without intent variance. Fix: cost circuit-breaker that re-routes to `gpt-5-nano` when confidence low.
- **Provider-missing hard failure.** `LLM.__new__` raises `ImportError` for missing native SDK (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:491`) or `LiteLLM not installed` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:494`) with no attempt at cheaper installed alternative. Fix: try cheap tier on `ImportError`, not just on API error.
- **Mutating shared options.** Middleware that does `options["model"] = "gpt-4o-mini"` in place risks leaking into tool-loop iterations — agent-framework doc footgun (`python/packages/openai/agent_framework_openai/_chat_client.py:778`). Fix: copy dict.
- **Wrong-model reissue after pin missing.** Metadata pin references a `model_id` not in chain (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:470` → falls through silently to normal retry). Symptom: re-issued request against wrong model. Fix: log/error on missing pin rather than fallthrough.
- **Billing staleness hiding cost.** `modelMatchLocalCache` TTL 10s + Redis TTL after pricing update (`worker/src/services/IngestionService/index.ts:1367` vs `web/src/server/api/routers/models.ts:449`) causes preview vs ingested cost divergence. Symptom: routing based on preview price makes wrong tier choice.
- **Naming collision pre-condition.** Adding `Router`/`router` in CrewAI or LangGraph without scoping collides with flow router (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/dsl/_router.py:97`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/state.py:982`). Symptom: import/attribute shadow, tests pass but runtime routes wrong graph.

## Open Questions

- **Is cheap-first with classifier win actually net-cheap?** No source benchmarks classifier latency/cost vs. savings (agent-framework `Future Considerations` flag). Need measurement: does `flex` + cheap scout actually cut spend or add double-invocation + jitter?
- **Where should `Router` live?** CrewAI naming collision (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/dsl/_router.py:97`), langgraph drift to `langchain.agents.create_agent` (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/chat_agent_executor.py:311`), openhands delegation to `software-agent-sdk` (`AGENTS.md:31-36`) all suggest the right layer may be gateway/SDK rather than harness. Is there consensus on locus?
- **Do pricing tiers become routing tiers?** Langfuse already encodes thresholds (`input_tokens > 128k` surcharge, `service_tier in [fast, priority]` at `worker/src/constants/default-model-prices.json:5`) that could pre-emptively avoid expensive tiers — but no RFC links `matchPricingTier` to a future router. Should billing rules directly drive runtime choice?
- **Can replay-safety gates allow degraded retries?** openai-agents-sdk vetoes (`src/agents/run_internal/model_retry.py:389`) intentionally block stale-state replay; a `fallback_to_tier` helper that returns `RetryDecision{fallback_model}` would need reconciliation — is safe downgrade only allowed for non-stateful, non-side-effecting calls?
- **Is OpenRouter `sort: price | throughput` the intended cost router?** pydantic-ai (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/openrouter.py:195`) and crewai/OpenAI SDK both have passthroughs to it, yet it bypasses self-hosted/local users — is gateway-delegated routing a deliberate split or accidental gap?
- **What latency/quality SLO defines "good enough" cheap?** No source declares `LATENCY_SLO` or `quality_floor`; langfuse's only timeout is `LANGFUSE_FETCH_LLM_COMPLETION_TIMEOUT_MS` (`packages/shared/src/env.ts:516`, capped 95s in `packages/shared/src/server/llm/llmText.ts:64`). Without SLO, any router has no objective function.
- **Do enterprise tiers exist out of tree?** CrewAI `plus_api.py`/`CREWAI_ENTERPRISE_DEFAULT_OAUTH2_*` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/constants.py:5`), openhands `software-agent-sdk` (isolated, not inspected), langfuse self-host vs cloud custom models (`packages/shared/src/server/llm/types.ts:465`) — mature routers may be commercial-only, invisible to this study.
- **How to preserve routing history in OTel without span explosion?** pydantic-ai overwrites `gen_ai.request.model` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/models/fallback.py:477`) to keep one span coherent; alternative is child span per attempt or dedicated `gen_ai.routing.decision` event. Which survives sampling and stays joinable to cost dashboards?
