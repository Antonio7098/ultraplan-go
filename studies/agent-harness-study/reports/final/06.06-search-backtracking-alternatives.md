## Executive Summary

None of the four frameworks implements algorithmic search over alternative plans (beam, A*, MCTS) or a pluggable scoring/ranking registry for competing branches. Instead each emulates "try alternatives" through a different combination of **deterministic graph routing**, **checkpoint forking**, and **bounded retry loops**.

**LangGraph (7/10)** provides the only clear, tested substrate for search: deterministic `BranchSpec`/`Send`/`Command.goto` branching, isolated `PregelExecutableTask` parallelism under Bulk Synchronous Parallel (BSP) supersteps, and durable checkpoint time-travel with `get_state_history`/`update_state`/`fork` semantics and retained `ERROR` pending writes. Scoring is intentionally left to user code.

The other three (all 4/10) demonstrate fragments: **agent-framework** routes via `EdgeCondition`/`SwitchCaseEdgeGroup`/`FanOutEdgeGroup`, restores linearly via `WorkflowCheckpoint`+`previous_checkpoint_id`, and approximates replanning via `AgentLoopMiddleware` judge loops and `MagenticProgressLedger` stall→reset→replan; **crewai** forks `RuntimeState` onto a named `branch` namespace with branch-partitioned `JsonProvider`/`SqliteProvider` and races Flow `or_` listeners; **openhands** deep-copies `LocalConversation` via `fork()` and fans out parallel sub-agents via `DelegateExecutor`/`TaskManager`, gated by a single `CriticResult(score)` threshold. All lack coexisting-plan comparison, global cost budgets, and tree-structured backtracking — alternatives are tried sequentially or manually, costs are capped per subsystem, and failed branches are observed but not ranked.

The core lesson: isolation and time-travel are solved; **selection among alternatives is not**.

## Core Thesis

Across all sources the dimension converges on a **routing-plus-checkpoint** model rather than a **search** model. Branching is expressed as data-dependent graph edges evaluated once per superstep/step; backtracking is implemented as restoring a serialized snapshot and re-executing forward. Parallelism exists (BSP Tasks, FanOut, racing `or_`, ThreadPool delegation), but it is **unranked parallelism** — every successor is equally valid until user code imposes preference. Cost controls are uniformly **iteration-bounded**, not token/price/wall-time bounded.

Durable time-travel with explicit fork semantics (LangGraph, CrewAI's branch namespace, OpenHands' deep-copy fork, Agent-Framework's linear restore) answers "can we recover from a bad path?" affirmatively. Whether the system remembers what it learned after recovery diverges: Magentic's `StallDetector` and `TodoList.replace_pending_todos` inject new facts/instructions, AgentLoop's `progress` log persists across `fresh_context` resets, and CrewAI/OpenHands copy full `EventRecord`/`EventLog` — but none retain failed branches as comparable candidates with scores. Divergence reflects product constraints: LangGraph's channel-state BSP emphasizes reproducibility and replay; Agent-Framework's workflow tries to serialize callables for durability; CrewAI/OpenHands prioritize manual A/B experimentation over automated planning search.

> Frameworks give you branches and checkpoints; you must build the search.

## Approach Models

**Model 1 — Workflow-Graph Routing (agent-framework, langgraph-core).**
Branching is a predicate evaluated at an edge, dispatching to exactly one or a subset of successors. Agent-framework uses `EdgeCondition: Callable[[Any], bool]` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:21-22`, `SwitchCaseEdgeGroup` sequential dispatch at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:649-752`, and `FanOutEdgeGroup(selection_func)` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:499-612`. LangGraph uses `BranchSpec(path, ends)` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/_branch.py:83-225` for static edges and `Send`/`Command.goto` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:704-831` for dynamic fan-out. Execution is BSP supersteps (`RunnerImpl.run_until_convergence` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:106-177`; LangGraph `plan->execution->update` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:464-476`). Strength: deterministic, observable. Weakness: enumerates routes, does not score them.

**Model 2 — Checkpoint Branch-Namespace (crewai, openhands, agent-framework-checkpoints).**
Alternatives coexist as named branches off a persisted snapshot. CrewAI's `RuntimeState.fork(branch)` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/runtime.py:352-389` writes to `location/branch/{ts}...json` via `JsonProvider` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/provider/json_provider.py:42-111`. OpenHands' `LocalConversation.fork()` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:314-415` deep-copies events via `model_copy(deep=True)` and creates a new persistence dir. Agent-framework's `WorkflowCheckpoint{previous_checkpoint_id}` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:85` plus `InProcRunnerContext.apply_checkpoint` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner_context.py:498-514` does copy-on-restore linear lineage (no tree). LangGraph's fork-on-read via `is_time_traveling`→`_put_checkpoint(source="fork")` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:952-972` and `__copy__`+`bulk_update_state` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1800-1825` is the most durable variant. Strength: isolation and replay. Weakness: except LangGraph, branches are manual and unranked.

**Model 3 — Iterative Retry/Replan Loop (agent-framework loops, openhands critic, crewai Todo refinement).**
A single trajectory is retried until a predicate or score says stop, optionally replanning. Agent-framework's `AgentLoopMiddleware(should_continue, max_iterations=10, fresh_context)` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:119-128` and `with_judge` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:506-600`; OpenHands' `CriticMixin._check_iterative_refinement` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/critic_mixin.py:76-138` with `CriticBase.evaluate()->CriticResult(score)` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:20-54`; CrewAI's `AgentReasoning._refine_plan_if_needed` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/utilities/reasoning_handler.py:244-349` and `TodoList.replace_pending_todos` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/utilities/planning_types.py:185-196`; LangGraph's `RetryPolicy` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:418-437` and per-node `error_handler_node` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:1110-1248`. Distinctively, agent-framework's `MagenticProgressLedger{is_request_satisfied, is_in_loop, is_progress_being_made}` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:289-335` and `MagenticContext{stall_count, reset_count}` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:337-395` triggers explicit `_reset_and_replan` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:1116-1193` — the only stall-detect→replan that updates facts/plan. Strength: local recovery without branching. Weakness: sequential, discards failed branch evidence, risks burning reset budget on parse failures.

**Model 4 — Parallel Task Fan-Out as Implicit Search (partial in all).**
Multiple candidates executed in one superstep/thread-pool batch, winner picked by first-completion or parent LLM. LangGraph `Send`-fan-out at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:442-467`; Agent-framework `FanOutEdgeGroup` with runner parallel supersteps at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:179-229`; CrewAI racing `or_` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:1098-1221`; OpenHands `DelegateExecutor.delegate` threading at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:254-384` and `ParallelToolExecutor` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/parallel_executor.py:38-92`. All choose by arrival order or string concatenation (`Agent {id}: {result}`), not score — LangGraph and OpenHands acknowledge the LLM parent must interpret.

## Pattern Catalog

**1. Conditional Edge / BranchSpec routing.**
Problem: choose among successors without enumerating plans. Sources: agent-framework (`EdgeCondition`, `SwitchCaseEdgeGroup`), langgraph (`BranchSpec`), crewai Flow `@router`/`or_`/`and_`. Why it works: predicate evaluated per message, keeps graph declarative and serializable (`to_dict`/`from_dict` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:396-465`). Copy when workflow is DAG-shaped and branching is rule-based. Overkill when branching depends on LLM judgment over multiple candidates — use dynamic Send/selection_func instead. Risk: deserialized `_missing_callable` proxy raises at runtime (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:50-72`). Evidence: `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:75-198`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/_branch.py:83-225`, `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/dsl/_router.py:97-162`.

**2. Selection-function indirection for multi-target dispatch.**
Problem: let caller decide which subset fires without baking scoring into engine. Sources: agent-framework `FanOutEdgeGroup(selection_func)` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:503-535` and `GroupChatOrchestrator(selection_func)` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_group_chat.py:73-95`, crewai `Flow` runtime, openhands `TaskToolSet`. Why it works: decouples routing policy from runner. Copy when you need conditional parallelism. Risk: untyped stringly selectors, no validation until superstep. Evidence: as above plus `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:1098-1221`.

**3. Dynamic Send / TaskTool fan-out.**
Problem: data-dependent parallelism (map-reduce, candidate generation). Sources: langgraph `Send(node, arg)` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:704-792` creating PUSH tasks at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:442-467`; agent-framework `FanOutEdgeGroup`; openhands `DelegateExecutor.spawn`/`delegate` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:41-50`. Why it works: tasks isolated by `task_path` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:1412-1420` and `writes: deque` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:616-756`; `TASKS` Topic at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:809` separates control. Copy for bounded map-reduce. Overkill/risky: no `max_branching` guard — unbounded n-Send explodes until `GraphRecursionError` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:3005-3012`. Evidence: `libs/langgraph/tests/test_pregel.py:1173-1217`, `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/task/manager.py:144-182`.

**4. Checkpoint time-travel fork.**
Problem: try alternatives from same ancestor without overwriting history. Sources: langgraph `__copy__` fork + `is_time_traveling` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:952-972`, crewai `RuntimeState.fork(branch)` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/runtime.py:352-389` with branch-aware providers, openhands `LocalConversation.fork()` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:314-415`, agent-framework linear restore at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:278-389`. Why it works: creates new lineage (`source=fork`, `parent_id`, `fork/{uuid}`) and isolates writes. Copy when experiments need auditability. LangGraph is durable and git-like; crewai/openhands require manual divergent inputs. Agent-framework's `previous_checkpoint_id` linear chain at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:85` is intentionally not a tree — copying it for search requires adding branch tree externally. Evidence: `libs/langgraph/tests/test_pregel.py:592-669`, `lib/crewai/tests/test_checkpoint.py:285-308`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:30-98`.

**5. Predicate/judge retry loop.**
Problem: iterative improvement on one branch without forking. Sources: agent-framework `AgentLoopMiddleware(should_continue, max_iterations=10)` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:216-420`, openhands `CriticMixin` + `CriticBase` scoring at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:20-54`, crewai `guardrail_max_retries=3` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/task.py:279-282` and `PlanningConfig.max_attempts`. Why it works: feedback string injected via `record_feedback`/`inject_progress` or critic follow-up `MessageEvent`. Copy for local correctness gating. Risk: sequential, can burn budget on malformed LLM JSON (magentic `progress_ledger_retry_count=3` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:588-590` then stall→reset). Evidence: `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:443-476`, `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/critic_mixin.py:76-138`.

**6. Stall-detect replan (Magentic ledger).**
Problem: detect non-progress and generate a fresh plan that avoids prior mistakes. Distinctive source: agent-framework `MagenticProgressLedger` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:289-335` with `_handle_messages` stall logic at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:1105-1155` and `MagenticContext` counters at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:337-395`. Why it works: LLM self-scores `is_progress_being_made`/`is_in_loop` with reasons, triggers `_reset_and_replan` broadcast at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:1156-1193`. Copy when multi-agent collaboration stalls — ledger is the closest thing to learned scoring among sources. Overkill for single-agent loops; requires prompt discipline and retry budget (3). Evidence: `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:469-512`, `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:1229-1265`.

**7. Racing first-wins selection.**
Problem: when multiple triggers fire, pick one deterministically. Sources: crewai `_build_racing_groups`/`_execute_racing_listeners` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:1098-1221`, agent-framework synchronous rejection `WorkflowException("already running")` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_workflow.py:768-791`, langgraph `sorted(triggered_nodes)` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:475-512`. Why it works: prevents double-firing. Copy when races are truly competing. Risk: picks fastest, not best — conflates latency with quality.

**8. Retained failed-branch trace.**
Problem: audit and learn from bad paths. Sources: langgraph `ERROR` pending_writes + `_read_error_for_task_id_from_pending_writes` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:780-797` with durability via `_delta_write_futs` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:201-213`; crewai `EventRecord{EventNode edges: parent|child|trigger|next}` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/event_record.py:64-146` with checkpoint events; openhands append-only `EventLog` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/event_store.py:44-153`; agent-framework `WorkflowEvent.failed` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_workflow.py:600-623`. Copy when post-hoc analysis matters. Gap: none stores failed branch + score for comparison; agent-framework and crewai discard pending plan context on replan.

## Key Differences

**Branching cardinality and determinism.** Agent-framework `SwitchCaseEdgeGroup` guarantees exactly-one dispatch and sequential condition evaluation with a required `Default` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:806-905`). LangGraph's `BranchSpec` and `Send` list allow one-to-many, creating N parallel PUSH tasks next superstep (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:938-1107`). CrewAI's `or_` racing is exclusive first-wins (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:1206-1217`), while OpenHands' `DelegateExecutor` fans out N threads and joins all (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:286-338`). Contingency: single-dispatch suits deterministic handoffs; fan-out suits map-reduce; racing suits competing triggers where only one should fire.

**Isolation mechanism.** LangGraph isolates per `PregelExecutableTask` with own `id` (xxhash of checkpoint+step+name at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:989-996`), own `writes`, own `config`/`scratchpad`, and BSP bulk-sync applying writes next superstep at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:232-345`. Agent-framework holds single `_messages` dict and single `_pending_request_info_events` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner_context.py:324,334`; restore overwrites (no COW tree). CrewAI partitions storage by `branch` string but shares in-memory views via fresh `Memory()` fallback at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:540-571` — isolation is durable but in-memory context is lost. OpenHands gives full `FIFOLock` + `EventLog` dir isolation per `LocalConversation` but shares `working_dir` filesystem at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:157,189-196`, so file races are possible unless `ResourceLockManager` respected. Valid response to tradeoffs: BSP for determinism, shared-workspace for fast file sharing, branch-namespace for simple experimentation.

**Backtracking explicitness.** LangGraph exposes the richest API: `get_state_history` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1480-1531`, `get_state(checkpoint_id)` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1392-1434`, `update_state`/`bulk_update_state` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1590-2054`, and `invoke(None, {checkpoint_id})` fork detection at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:874-972`. Agent-framework exposes `Workflow.run(checkpoint_id, checkpoint_storage)` and `restore_from_checkpoint` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:278-389` plus per-superstep `create_checkpoint_if_enabled` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:108-114`, but lineage is linear (`previous_checkpoint_id`). CrewAI exposes `from_checkpoint` then `fork(branch)` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:453-476` with `checkpoint_kickoff_event_id` resume at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/crews/utils.py:143-152`. OpenHands has no `backtrack()`/`undo()` — fork-as-checkpoint plus `reject_pending_actions` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:896-926` are workarounds.

**Scoring and comparison.** All lack it, but degree differs. Agent-framework's `MagenticProgressLedger` scores current trajectory against task (not alternatives) at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:308-334`; `GroupChatOrchestrator(selection_func)` returns single speaker name at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_group_chat.py:73-95`. CrewAI defines `TaskEvaluation.quality 0-10` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/utilities/evaluators/task_evaluator.py:28-37` but only for offline `Crew.train()`. OpenHands' `CriticResult(score, success)` gates retry via `score < success_threshold` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:20-54` — single-branch gating, not ranking. LangGraph has no scoring primitive at all (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:475-483` only deterministic trigger checks). None stores `BranchEvaluation` or comparator.

**Cost bounding granularity.** All bound wall-clock iterations, none bound tokens/cost globally. LangGraph's three layers are most complete: `recursion_limit` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:2563-2564` + `RetryPolicy.max_attempts=3` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:418-437` + `TimeoutPolicy` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:452-514`. Agent-framework has fragmented caps: `Workflow DEFAULT_MAX_ITERATIONS=100` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_const.py:4`, `AgentLoop DEFAULT_MAX_ITERATIONS=10` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:122-127`, Magentic `max_stall=3`/`max_reset`/`max_round` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_magentic.py:475-482`, GroupChat `max_rounds` at `python/packages/orchestrations/agent_framework_orchestrations/_group_chat.py:118-126`. CrewAI uses `Flow.max_method_calls=100` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:614` plus `max_checkpoints` pruning at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/checkpoint_listener.py:191-216` but pruning limits storage not API cost. OpenHands uses coarse `max_iteration_per_run=500` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:106-111`, `max_children=5` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:44`, `StuckDetector` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/stuck_detector.py:24-138`.

## Tradeoffs

**Expressiveness vs serializability.** Declarative edge conditions serialize to `condition_name`/`selection_func_name` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:441-449` enabling `Workflow.to_dict` JSON durability. Cost: after `from_dict` they become `_missing_callable` proxies that `RuntimeError` on invocation at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:50-72`. LangGraph avoids this by requiring callables at compile time; durability is via checkpoint values, not graph definition.

**Durability vs complexity/storage.** Full `RuntimeState` (entities+`EventRecord`) per write at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/runtime.py:190-220` and per-superstep `WorkflowCheckpoint` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:165-166` give reliable resume but O(n) copy and unbounded growth — `InMemoryCheckpointStorage` is an unbounded dict at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:207` and `FileCheckpointStorage` never evicts. LangGraph similarly retains every checkpoint with no GC (`checkpointer.list` has limit but no policy). Mitigated by `max_checkpoints` per branch at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/provider/json_provider.py:98-111`.

**BSP determinism vs latency/throughput.** LangGraph's `apply_writes` barrier at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:232-345` and agent-framework supersteps at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:129-135` (50ms poll) prevent races and make backtracking sound, but force parallel sends to synchronize per superstep. OpenHands' `ThreadPoolExecutor` + `ResourceLockManager` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/parallel_executor.py:38-92` allows freer concurrency but risks file-level races when locks undeclared.

**Generality vs out-of-box search.** Not shipping a scorer keeps LangGraph small and avoids opinionated search semantics, but forces every planning agent to reinvent ranking and risks inconsistent implementations. Agent-framework's Magentic ledger is opinionated but scoped to one orchestrator; reusing it elsewhere requires copying prompts. CrewAI/OpenHands' single-threshold critics are simple but insufficient for multi-objective or Pareto ranking.

**Availability vs backtracking fidelity.** Agent-framework's `create_checkpoint_if_enabled` swallows failures with warn-and-continue at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:268-276`, keeping workflow live but silently shrinking the backtracking window. CrewAI's `is_replaying()` guard at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/checkpoint_listener.py:231-232` avoids infinite loops but suppresses checkpoint writes while not suppressing `usage_metrics` side effects. Hard-failing would improve fidelity at cost of availability.

## Decision Guide

**Choose LangGraph as substrate when:** you need reproducible branching with durable audit trails and explicit time-travel. It is the only framework where `get_state_history` + `update_state` + fork-on-read gives git-like experimentation with retained failure evidence. Add your own `BranchScorer` outside the engine (`ScorePolicy` over `Sequence[PregelTask]`) and a `max_concurrent_sends` guard. Set `recursion_limit` and `RetryPolicy.max_attempts`/`TimeoutPolicy` deliberately — they are your only engine-level cost bounds.

**Choose Agent-Framework workflow + Magentic when:** your system is a structured workflow with HIL checkpoints and multi-agent chat that occasionally stalls. Use `SwitchCase`/`FanOut` for rule-based routing, `WorkflowCheckpoint` for HIL resume across restarts, and Magentic's ledger+stall loop for collaboration recovery. Do not try to build beam search inside `FanOut` — you will hit linear lineage and `_missing_callable` after `from_dict`. For iterative single-agent improvement, use `AgentLoopMiddleware.with_judge` rather than reinventing Magentic.

**Choose CrewAI branch forking when:** you need lightweight manual A/B experiments from a persisted snapshot without a full graph engine. Call `from_checkpoint` then `fork("experiment")` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:453-476`, supply divergent `inputs`, and rely on branch-partitioned providers. Budget for large JSON writes (full `EventRecord`) and enforce your own `max_branches` external to `max_checkpoints`. Remember `Memory` will reset to fresh `Memory()` on restore — rehydrate learned context explicitly if needed.

**Choose OpenHands forking/delegation when:** alternatives are whole-conversation explorations (different prompts, agents, or `subagent_type`) where filesystem persistence matters. Prefer `TaskManager`/`TaskToolSet` over legacy `DelegateExecutor` (deprecated per `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:1`). Isolate file mutations via `ResourceLockManager` declared resources, or give each branch a snapshot workspace if correctness matters more than sharing. Use `CriticBase` only as a single-branch gate — do not expect it to rank N branches.

**General rule for scoring alternatives:**

- If selection depends on rule predicates → use conditional edge / BranchSpec.
- If selection depends on quality of candidate outputs → generate candidates via `Send`/fork, then score in user code before emitting next `Send`/`Command`.
- If recovery depends on detecting stagnation → use ledger/stall counters with reason fields, not bare retry counts.
- If you need N candidates compared → do not rely on racing first-wins; it is latency-based, not quality-based.

## Practical Tips

**Patterns to copy:**

- **BSP isolated tasks.** LangGraph's per-task `writes: deque` + `task_path` + next-superstep `apply_writes` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:616-756` prevents cross-branch corruption and makes replay deterministic. Copy the isolation even if you don't copy the engine — one deque + bulk-sync barrier eliminates whole classes of races.
- **Progress log as continuity carrier.** Agent-framework's `fresh_context` snapshot + `_render_progress` list[str] injection at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_loop.py:462-476,798-802` and `todos_remaining` pattern keeps learning across fresh-context resets. Reuse when you reset history but want to retain facts.
- **Branch-aware pruning.** CrewAI's `provider.prune(location, max_keep, branch)` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/provider/json_provider.py:98-111` keeps experiments from evicting `main`. Add per-lineage retention wherever you add forking.
- **Exit-mode durability tradeoff.** LangGraph's `_exit_delta_writes` accumulator at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_checkpoint.py:39` skips intermediate checkpoints while preserving write order — apply when durability per step is too costly but ordering must survive.
- **Explicit fork cost isolation.** OpenHands' `reset_metrics=True` default on fork at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:321,404-406` and branch-isolated persistence dir give clean accounting per alternative. Adopt for cost attribution.

**Patterns to avoid or delay:**

- **Shared `working_dir` for parallel branches** (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/tools/delegate/impl.py:157`) — fine for read-heavy sharing, unsafe for write-heavy exploration without per-branch filesystem snapshots.
- **Racing `or_` as quality selector** (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:1206-1217`) — delay using it until you accept latency-wins semantics; otherwise add explicit scoring after race.
- **Per-subsystem caps without global budget** — all sources demonstrate that `recursion_limit`/`max_iterations`/`max_children` alone cannot bound token/\$ across forks. Do not launch fan-out loops without an external token counter and fan-out limiter.
- **Persisting live callables in checkpoints** — Agent-framework's `condition_name` proxy trap at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_edge.py:50-72` shows why: register callables in an explicit registry before `from_dict`.

**Decision rules:**

- Need auditability across restarts? Require durable `ERROR`/`pending_writes` + fork-on-read (LangGraph model). Anything else needs custom write-ahead.
- Need N candidates ranked? Budget for user-space scorer + `select_best` that consumes candidate outputs before next superstep. No source provides this — plan to build it.
- Need to recover without forgetting? Prefer ledger-with-reasons (Magentic) or `EventRecord` DAG over bare retry counts; reasons survive replan prompts.
- Need many short-lived experiments? CrewAI branch namespace is cheapest to operate; LangGraph checkpoint storage is most robust.

**Caution signs the design is becoming brittle:**

- Fan-out count or `Send` list grows without a `max_concurrent_sends` check (LangGraph `TASKS` channel unbounded).
- `FileCheckpointStorage` or `EventLog` file count grows linearly with supersteps/tasks with no `prune` or `max_checkpoints` enforcement (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:384-421`).
- `create_checkpoint_if_enabled` warnings appear in logs but runs report success (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:268-276`) — backtracking fidelity is silently degraded.
- Restored workflow throws `_missing_callable` or `Ambiguous update` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1934`) at first conditional edge — registry not rehydrated.
- `is_replaying()` suppresses checkpoints while `usage_metrics` still accrues (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/checkpoint_listener.py:231-232` vs `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:918-925`) — double-counting on replay.

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **Linear lineage masquerading as branching.** `previous_checkpoint_id` chaining at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:85` plus `clear()+import_state` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:324-325` discards abandoned branch state. Do not treat sequential `restore`+`run` as parallel alternatives.
- **Silently truncated non-convergence.** `WorkflowConvergenceException` only fires if `has_messages()` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner.py:176-177`; an idle graph that never converges can return success with incomplete outputs.
- **Fork without monotonic branch guarantee.** CrewAI auto-branch `uuid4().hex[:8]` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/runtime.py:365-368` risks collision; OpenHands `model_copy(deep=True)` loop at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:385-386` is O(n) — neither scales to thousands of branches without counters or COW.
- **Stuck history inheritance.** Forking a `STUCK` or `FINISHED` conversation copies stuck-inducing history verbatim at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/impl/local_conversation.py:314-415`; child may immediately re-trigger `StuckDetector` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/stuck_detector.py:24-138` unless you prune history.
- **Unbounded checkpoints on NFS.** `EventLog` `flock` unreliable on NFS at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/event_store.py:30-35`; `FileCheckpointStorage` atomic json+pickle can still interleave under concurrent fork/task writes.
- **Overloading racing for ranking.** Using `or_` first-wins or `Thread.join` order as implicit ranking confuses latency with quality — add explicit evaluation (`CriticBase`/`TaskEvaluation` must be wired to a selector, not just logged).
- **Swallowed critic failures.** `critic.evaluate` exception returns `None` → `should_refine=False` at `studies/agent-harness-study/sources/openhands/_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/critic_mixin.py:72-74` — failed branch silently finishes as success.

## Notable Absences

Across all sources no evidence of:

- Pluggable `ScorePolicy`/`BranchScorer` interface, beam width, or best-first queue. Closest is `MagenticProgressLedger` scoring the current trajectory and `CriticResult` gating retry — neither ranks N coexisting branches. Verified by empty scoring grep in langgraph at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:475-483`.
- Global token/\$/wall-time budget spanning multiple branches or forks. All bounds are per-run or per-subsystem (`recursion_limit`, `max_iterations`, `max_children`, `max_attempts`, `max_checkpoints`).
- Branch GC or retention policy beyond CrewAI's `max_checkpoints` per branch and LangGraph's manual `checkpointer.list(limit)` — no `prune_checkpoints(before, keep=N forks)` or `max_forks` per thread.
